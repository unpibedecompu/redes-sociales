- Anthropic is likely improving the capabilities of their models on well-specified tasks at around twice the rate they did between March 2024 and October 2025.
- A majority of this acceleration today is likely attributable to Anthropic’s extensive use of AI to do R&D
- The lack of observed bottlenecks or strong diminishing returns means there is a significant risk that further capabilities progress could lead to even faster acceleration
- We are confident that the acceleration was not due to an acceleration in the scaling of training compute.
- Interviews and surveys are consistent with AI usage causing a doubling in the pace of capabilities progress.
- We were told that R&D is accelerated primarily via AI assistance rather than fully autonomous contributions, but there are some signs that fully autonomous contributions will play a larger role soon.

- Attribution:
  - The acceleration in late 2025 was likely influenced by non-AI factors.
  - We guess that a majority (perhaps ≈80%) of the current (October 2026) acceleration is due to AI.

- Bottleneck:
  - A key concern is that acceleration from the use of AI could start to compound, as the development of more capable models leads to further acceleration.
  - we did not identify any clear bottlenecks (limited inputs that would prevent further growth) or steep diminishing returns to further scaling that would get in the way of such compounding acceleration
