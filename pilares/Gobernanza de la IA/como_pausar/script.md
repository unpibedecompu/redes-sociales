## Step 1: compute declaration

- Training requires a ton of chips
- Datacenters are visible from space
- Datacenters require infraestructure for power
- chips are manufactured at a handful of fabrication plants at Taiwan, South Korea, US and China

Then:
- Each major actor on the supply chain (datacenter owners, suppliers, chips fabricators) have to declare their major purchases and sales.
- Internal and international inspectors

Result: everything is monitored and accounted for.

## Step 2: pause training

- Temporary pause on all new training runs
- Inference for current models is great
- Adversary verification devices are used for this pause -> check not training, inference only
- Assuption: verification devices exists

Result: 
- everyone can safely halt training
- There is time to create fine-grained verification options for training to restart

## Step 3: Get Worldwide Buy-in

- Initialize as a Bilateral deal between US and China
- Some countries that were worried accept the deal
- Pax Silica and WAICO
- They want US and China to proceed slowly and transparently
- The "Consortium" is created

## 2030: Plan A is Established

- AI development continues in a more cautious, transparent and distriubted way.
