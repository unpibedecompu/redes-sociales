- Training requires a ton of chips
- Datacenters are visible from space

- chips are manufactured at a handful of fabrication plants at Taiwan, South Korea, US and China

Then:
- Each major actor on the supply chain (datacenter owners, suppliers, chips fabricators) have to declare their major purchases and sales.
- Internal and international inspectors

Result: everything is monitored and accounted for.

## Plan A principles:

### Principle 1: Buy Time

- There is a high uncertainty that has to be solve before an intelligence explosion. This could cause:
    - AI dominance
    - power concentration on presidents and CEO's
    - WW3
    - Maybe everything goes well
- Currently no one know if AI are trusworthy, or how to regulate it

### Principle 2: Total Research Transparency

- If countries on consotium see research they do not like, they can ban it.
- Open Algorithm and open access to weight for evaluations (not for productive activities)
- Research is transparent, inference is private
- Rival companies can raise alarm on each other for dangerous practices
- Difficult secret loyalties
- No incentive to race for the companies

### Principle 3: Diffuse AI Broadly

- Reached by Principle 1 and 2

### Principle 4: Reversivility

- The datacenter created could be use to faster accelerate the race in case of deal dissolution
- compute must be destroyed in case of deal dissolution
- U.S. builds datacenter in Mongolia
- China builds datacenter in Canada
- In case of dissolution both take over the rival datacenter and remotly destroy their own before it fall on the rival hands -> "Mutually Assured Compute Destruction"
