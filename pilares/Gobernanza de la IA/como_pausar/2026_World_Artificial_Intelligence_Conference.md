# Summary
- how to promote AI globally for the positive, for good and for humanity.
- How to get along with thinking machines? How to ensure security when algorithm is part of decision making? How to tackle ethical challenges by technologies through adaptive governance? How to realize AI for all when the divide keeps widening? These questions demand serious consideration and real answers from the whole international community.

Four points:

- First:
    - encourage open source, openness, collaboration and sharing.
- Second:
    - we should strengthen risk-awareness and ensure that AI is secure and controllable.
    - We should take seriously the various types of inherent and secondary risks that AI may trigger.
    - ensure that AI is always under human control.
- Third:
    - we should encourage inclusiveness and promote mutual learning between civilizations.
    -  We must shape the values of AI with humanity's common values
- Fourth:
    - improve global governance
    -  We should enhance alignment and coordination on AI development strategies, governance rules and technical standards, so as to form a consensus-based global governance framework at an early date to make this frontier technology better benefit humanity.
    - extensive international cooperation

- AI global governance proposals:
    - Global AI Governance Initiative
    - AI Capacity-Building Action Plan for Good and for All
    - AI Plus International Cooperation Initiative
    - World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO) -> it exists currently
    - All these make enphasis on the global south participation and true multilateralism

- AI development should not be a solo performance by a single country, but a symphony of international cooperation
- We must make its oversight and governance precise and effective, and constantly refine measures to forestall loss of control.
- We are ready to work with all parties to seize the opportunities of AI development and meet the challenges
